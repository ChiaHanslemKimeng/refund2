Welcome to Identity Verification (KYC)

Please review the compliance explanation and accept the verification terms to proceed.

Why is Identity Verification (KYC) Required?

At Nexis Cyber Recovery, we specialize in high-stakes forensic investigation, international chargebacks, and legal asset restitution. To ensure strict compliance with international banking regulations and safeguard all parties, completing our Know Your Customer (KYC) process is mandatory before initiating any transactions or lodging recovery claims.

International AML & CFT Compliance

Under global financial protocols (FATF, FinCEN, EU 5th AMLD), fund recovery agencies must verify the legal identity of claimants to prevent fraudulent money laundering and illegal cross-border disbursements.

Claimant Protection & Safe Payouts

KYC verification ensures that recovered funds, settlement payouts, and card chargebacks are disbursed solely to the verified, rightful claimant, eliminating synthetic identity theft.

Card & Bank Dispute Standing

When filing formal chargebacks under Visa Core Rules or Mastercard Chargeback Guide, acquiring banks demand certified proof of claimant identity and verified payment card ownership.

Enforceable Evidence Records

Verified identity files provide legal standing required by financial ombudsmen and regulatory arbiters to order swift merchant restitution.

KYC Verification Terms & Conditions

1. Accuracy and Authenticity of Information

You hereby attest under penalty of perjury that all documents, identity credentials, residential addresses, and credit or debit card details submitted during this verification procedure are genuine, authentic, current, and legally belong to you.

2. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Fraud Prevention

Nexis Cyber Recovery reserves the right to conduct electronic cross-referencing against global sanctions lists, PEP databases, and international fraud registries. Any fraudulent or counterfeit documentation will result in immediate disqualification and regulatory escalation.

3. Payment Card Authorization & Regulatory Fees

You acknowledge that a payment card is registered to verify account ownership and authorize recovery restitution channels. A statutory forensic validation or regulatory clearance fee may apply to finalize verification, as communicated by compliance administration.

4. Data Privacy & 256-Bit Encryption

All identity documents and card numbers are encrypted using AES-256 bank-level encryption and stored in compliant security vaults strictly in accordance with GDPR and international data protection laws.
