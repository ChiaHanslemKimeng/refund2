1
Tap the Share Button

2
Select "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the Share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add at top right.
3
Open from Home Screen & Enable
Open the new Nexis Admin app icon from your Home Screen. Tap Turn On Push Notifications to allow background alerts with vibration!
