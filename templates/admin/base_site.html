📱 Setup iOS Push Alerts (WhatsApp Style)

Apple iOS Safari requires adding this web portal to your Home Screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA) to unlock native background push notifications, custom sounds, and vibration.

1
Tap the Share Button
In Safari's toolbar at the bottom of your iPhone/iPad, tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing upward).
2
Select "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the Share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add at top right.
3
Open from Home Screen & Enable
Open the new Nexis Admin app icon from your Home Screen. Tap Turn On Push Notifications to allow background alerts with vibration!
